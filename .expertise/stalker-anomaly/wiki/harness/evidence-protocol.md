# Mandatory vault evidence protocol

Before a repository decision, the `stalker-anomaly-expert` harness must collect a concise evidence pack from the `stalker-anomaly` vault. The pack states freshness and version, Wiki routes, verified source paths and ranges, conflicts, and missing evidence; a stale or incomplete vault is disclosed or repaired rather than replaced by model memory.
